# 🚢 Titanic Survival Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This project explores the famous **Titanic dataset** using Python libraries such as Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. The goal is to extract insights and patterns that influenced passenger survival during the Titanic disaster.

## 🎯 Objective

Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand:
- What factors affected survival?
- How variables like age, class, fare, and gender correlate with survival?
- Discover meaningful relationships through visualizations and statistics.

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset used is the [Titanic dataset] which includes:
- Passenger details such as age, sex, class, fare, etc.
- Survival status (0 = No, 1 = Yes)

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python**
- **Pandas**
- **Matplotlib**
- **Seaborn**
- **Jupyter Notebook**

## 📊 EDA Highlights

Key insights derived from the analysis:

- **Gender**: Females had a much higher survival rate compared to males.
- **Passenger Class**: First-class passengers were more likely to survive than second or third class.
- **Age**: Children had better survival chances, showing prioritization in rescue.
- **Fare**: Higher fare-paying passengers (usually in better classes) had higher survival rates.
- **Family Size**: Passengers traveling with 1–2 family members had a better chance of survival compared to those traveling alone or with large families.
- **Correlation**: 'Fare' and 'Pclass' had a strong negative correlation; survival had moderate correlations with class and gender.

## 📈 Visualizations Included

- Count plots of survival based on gender and class
- Histograms of age and fare distributions
- Boxplots of fare and age grouped by survival
- Scatterplots showing family members aboard vs. survival
- Heatmaps and pairplots for correlation analysis
